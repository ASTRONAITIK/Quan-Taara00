# Quan-Taara

Quan-Taara is a physics and space observatory with an interactive astronomy interface, an ASTROPIX local physics guide, and a separate space-news page.

## Open the site

Keep `quantaaraPrototype.backup.html` and `news.html` in the same folder. Open `quantaaraPrototype.backup.html` in a web browser, then choose **Space News** in the header to open the separate news page. The news page retrieves recent headlines directly from The Space Devs Spaceflight News API and displays stories published during the previous 24 hours; an internet connection is needed for live headlines.

The pages are standalone HTML. Choose **Event Horizon Run** after Library in the observatory header to play the real-time 3D space flight game. Steer with **WASD** or the arrow keys, hold **F** or the on-screen **Fire** button to shoot asteroids, and use **Space** to warp. A rock impact ends the run; clear all three stages to finish. The scoreboard tracks your best score in that browser. On phones, use the on-screen movement, fire, and warp buttons.

The game includes 3D ship and planet models, with optional free NASA GLB models you can load from the game page. ASTROPIX currently uses its built-in local physics guide; a broader AI model can be connected later.
