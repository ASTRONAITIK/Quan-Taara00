Bring in free NASA 3D models

Download the GLB model from NASA, then use the model buttons here to load it into the game. The planet appears in the distant scene; the spacecraft replaces your flight ship. NASA lists its 3D resources as free to download and use.

You can save the GLB in the same folder as Quan-Taara, then click Load ship or Load planet and select that file. Choose the GLB format where NASA offers several downloads.
